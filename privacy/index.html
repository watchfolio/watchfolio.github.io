Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Watchfolio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Watchfolio mobile application.

Information We Collect

Watchfolio does not require account creation and does not collect personal information.

All watch data you enter — including photos, notes, brand details, and collection information — is stored locally on your device.

Data Storage

Your collection data remains on your device unless you choose to share content using iOS sharing features.

Watchfolio does not operate a backend server and does not transmit your personal collection data to external databases.

Analytics

Watchfolio does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising SDKs.

Third-Party Services

If you choose to share content (such as your “Mes Quatre Temps” collector card), that action is handled through Apple’s standard iOS share sheet and subject to the privacy policies of the destination platform you select.

Children’s Privacy

Watchfolio does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
