Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Watchfolio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Watchfolio mobile application.

Information You Provide

Watchfolio allows you to create a personal watch journal. Any information you enter — including photos, notes, brand details, and collection data — is stored on your device.

Data Collection

Watchfolio does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information.

The app does not sell user data and does not use advertising networks.

App Functionality & Technical Services

The app may communicate with platform services necessary for operation, such as app updates or basic diagnostic reporting provided by Apple or development tools. This information, if collected, is used solely to maintain and improve app performance and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Data Sharing

Watchfolio does not share your personal watch collection data with third parties. If you choose to share content using iOS sharing features, that action is handled by Apple’s share sheet and subject to the privacy policies of the destination platform you select.

Children’s Privacy

Watchfolio is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
