Watchfolio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Overview

Watchfolio is a private journal for watch collectors. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly.

Information You Provide

Any watch information you enter — including photos, notes, brand details, and collection data — is stored locally on your device.

Data Collection

Watchfolio does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information. The app does not sell user data and does not use advertising networks.

App Functionality & Technical Services

The app may communicate with platform services necessary for operation, such as app updates or basic diagnostic reporting. If such technical information is processed, it is used only to maintain and improve the app and not for advertising or tracking.

Data Sharing

Watchfolio does not share your personal watch collection data with third parties. If you choose to share content using iOS sharing features, that action is handled by Apple’s share sheet and is subject to the privacy policies of the destination platform you select.

Children’s Privacy

Watchfolio is intended for general audiences and is not directed toward children under 13.
